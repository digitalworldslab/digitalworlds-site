FAQ

Does Etsy charge fees on digital downloads?

Yes. The listing fee, 6.5% transaction fee and payment processing apply the same way. There is just no shipping.

Is the transaction fee charged on shipping?

Yes, 6.5% applies to the item price plus shipping and gift wrap you charge.

What are Offsite Ads fees?

If a buyer clicks an Etsy ad on another site and buys within 30 days, Etsy charges 15% (12% for shops above $10,000 a year), capped at $100 per order.
