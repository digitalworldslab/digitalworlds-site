How to make it last past February

  • Automate it. Set a standing transfer the day after payday.
  • Track it visually. Colouring in a square each week is surprisingly motivating.
  • Allow catch-up weeks. Missed a week? Add it to the next one rather than quitting.
  • Build the emergency fund first. One month of essentials stops a surprise bill from wiping out the challenge.
  • Save for known costs separately. Use sinking funds for birthdays and car repairs so the challenge money stays untouched.

Questions

How does the 52-week challenge work?

Week 1 you save 1, week 2 you save 2, and so on up to 52. By the end of the year that adds up to 1,378.

Can I do it in reverse?

Yes. Starting with 52 and going down makes the hardest weeks come in January, when motivation is highest, and the easy weeks land in December.

Where should I keep the money?

A separate savings account, ideally one that pays interest and is not linked to your debit card.
