How to Save €10,000 in 2027: A Month-by-Month Plan

€10,000 in a year sounds big. Broken down, it is €833 a month, €385 per biweekly paycheck or €192 a week. The trick is deciding where that money comes from before January starts.

Month-by-month plan

  1. January: set up a separate savings account and a standing transfer the day after payday.
  2. February: build a one-month emergency fund first, so a surprise bill doesn't wipe the plan out.
  3. March to May: cut the easy costs (subscriptions, phone, insurance) and move the difference into the transfer.
  4. June: half-year check. You should be near €5,000. If not, raise the transfer or lower the goal now, not in December.
  5. July to November: add side income and save any windfalls such as tax refunds or bonuses.
  6. December: pay for Christmas from a sinking fund, not from the €10k.

Questions

How much do I need to save a week to reach €10,000 in a year?

About €192 a week, €385 per biweekly paycheck or €833 a month.

What if €10,000 is too much?

Pick the plan you can keep: €5,000 is about €96 a week, and the classic 52-week challenge reaches €1,378. A smaller goal you finish beats a big one you drop in March.

Should I pay off debt or save first?

Build a small emergency fund first, then pay off high-interest debt such as credit cards, then save toward bigger goals. Card interest usually costs more than savings earn.
