How to set up your 2027 budget in 15 minutes

  1. Use take-home pay. The amount that actually lands in your account, not your salary before tax.
  2. Copy last month's real numbers. Your bank app shows what you spent on groceries, transport and eating out. Start from that, not from what you wish you spent.
  3. Add the yearly costs as monthly amounts. €600 for Christmas is €50 a month. Our sinking funds calculator works these out.
  4. Check the split. A common target is about 50% needs, 30% wants, 20% savings and debt. It is a guide, not a rule; high-rent cities often need 60/20/20.
  5. Fill in "Actual" at the end of the month. The difference between planned and actual is where next month's budget improves.

Questions

Is this budget template really free?

Yes. Fill it in on the page, print it or download the CSV. No sign-up is needed and nothing is uploaded; your numbers are stored only in your browser.

Does the CSV open in Google Sheets?

Yes. In Google Sheets choose File, Import, Upload and select the CSV. Excel opens it directly.

What is a good budget split?

A common starting point is 50% needs, 30% wants and 20% savings and debt repayment, adjusted to your rent and income.

Should I budget monthly or per paycheck?

If you are paid monthly, budget monthly. If you are paid weekly or every two weeks, a per-paycheck plan stops bills from clashing.
