How to Make a Bill Payment Calendar (and Stop Missing Due Dates)

Every due date where you can see it, so nothing sneaks up on you.

Late fees rarely happen because there's no money. They happen because a bill was due on a day nobody was looking. A bill calendar puts every due date where you can see it.

Step 1: list every bill with its due day and frequency

Go through two or three months of bank statements: the bills that only come every 2 months, every quarter or once a year are the ones people forget.

Step 2: put them on a month view

On the calendar, the 18th now shows phone + internet (70) and the 1st shows rent (900). Seeing the busy days lets you move money in before them, or ask a provider to move a due date closer to payday.

Step 3: tick each one off as paid

A paid tick box for each bill in each month it's due is the simplest way to stop paying twice or not at all.

Step 4: set aside for the yearly ones

The €480 car insurance is €40 a month if you put it aside monthly. That's a sinking fund, and it means March is no longer the expensive month.

Questions

What is a bill calendar?

A month view with each bill on the day it's due, plus the total due that day, so you can see busy days before they arrive.

How do I track bills that aren't monthly?

List the frequency with each bill (every 2 months, quarterly, yearly) and set aside a monthly amount for the big yearly ones.

Can I change a bill's due date?

Many providers let you move a due date closer to payday if you ask. It's worth trying for the biggest bills.
