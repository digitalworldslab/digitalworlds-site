Sinking Funds List: 15 Categories and How Much to Save Each Month

Most budgets break on the expenses that don't come every month. A sinking fund turns each of them into a small monthly amount.

The formula

Monthly amount = target ÷ months left. €600 for Christmas, 12 months away: €50 a month. €480 car insurance due in 8 months: €60 a month.

15 common sinking funds

  1. Christmas and gifts
  2. Birthdays
  3. Car repairs and servicing
  4. Car insurance and road tax
  5. Holidays
  6. Home repairs
  7. Annual subscriptions and memberships
  8. Back to school
  9. Medical and dental
  10. Pet costs and vet
  11. Clothing
  12. New phone or laptop
  13. Weddings and events you're invited to
  14. Furniture and appliances
  15. Annual fees (bank, accountant, licences)

Start with the three that hurt most last year. Add more once those are running.

Questions

What is a sinking fund?

Money you set aside a little at a time for a known future cost, like Christmas, car repairs or insurance, so it doesn't land on a credit card.

How do I calculate a sinking fund?

Divide the amount you'll need by the months until you need it. €600 for Christmas in 12 months is €50 a month.

Is a sinking fund the same as an emergency fund?

No. An emergency fund is for the unexpected. Sinking funds are for costs you can see coming.
