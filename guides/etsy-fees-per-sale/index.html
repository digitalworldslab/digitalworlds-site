How Much Does Etsy Take Per Sale? Fees Explained With a €20 Example

Every fee on a single sale, added up, so you can price for real profit.

Etsy charges several small fees on each sale. Each one looks harmless; together they can take more than a quarter of the price if the sale came through Offsite Ads.

Example: a €20 digital download

FeeRate (example)On €20
Listing fee$0.20 per listing≈ 0.19
Transaction fee6.5% of item + shipping1.30
Payment processinge.g. 4% + €0.30 (varies by country)1.10
Total, normal sale≈ 2.59 (13%)
Offsite Ads, if the sale came from an ad15% (12% for bigger shops)3.00
Total, Offsite Ads sale≈ 5.59 (28%)

Payment processing rates and any regulatory operating fee depend on your shop's country, and VAT may apply to the fees themselves. Check Etsy's fee page for your country and put those rates into your calculation.

Then add your own costs

For physical products, add materials, packaging and postage. A €20 candle that costs €6 to make and €4 to ship has much less left than the €17.41 after fees suggests.

3 ways to keep more of each sale

  1. Price from profit, not from competitors: work out what you need to keep, then add fees on top.
  2. Account for Offsite Ads: if you can't opt out, price so an ad sale is still worth it.
  3. Bundle: one €30 bundle pays one listing fee and one fixed processing fee instead of three.

Questions

What percentage does Etsy take?

In our €20 example about 13% on a normal sale, and about 28% when the sale came through Offsite Ads. Exact rates depend on your country.

What is Etsy's transaction fee?

6.5% of the item price plus shipping and gift wrapping.

Can I avoid Offsite Ads fees?

Smaller shops can opt out; shops above Etsy's sales threshold cannot. Price so an ad sale is still profitable.
