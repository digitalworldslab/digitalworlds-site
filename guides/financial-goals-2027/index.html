How to make a goal stick

  1. Give it a number and a date. The calculator does the rest.
  2. Order your goals. A small emergency fund first, then high-interest debt, then the rest. The snowball vs avalanche guide helps with the debt part.
  3. Automate the transfer for the day after payday.
  4. Track it monthly. A progress bar you can see is the cheapest motivation there is.

Questions

What are good financial goals for 2027?

Common ones are a starter emergency fund, paying off a credit card, saving for Christmas and holidays in advance, and investing a fixed amount every month.

How do I calculate how much to save per month?

Subtract what you already have from the goal, then divide by the number of months until your date. The calculator above also adds savings interest.

How many money goals should I have at once?

One to three. More than that and every goal moves too slowly to stay motivating.

Should I save or pay off debt first?

Most people do best with a small emergency fund first, then high-interest debt, then larger savings goals.
