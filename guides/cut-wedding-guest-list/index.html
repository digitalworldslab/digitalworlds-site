How to Cut Your Wedding Guest List (and What 30 Fewer Guests Saves)

The guest list drives almost every other cost. A simple method to shorten it fairly.

The guest list sets the size of almost every other wedding cost. Each extra guest adds a meal, a drink, a chair, a favour, and sometimes a bigger venue.

The A / B / C list method

  1. A list: people you can't imagine the day without. Invite them first.
  2. B list: people you'd like there. Invite them as A-list replies say no, early enough to be polite.
  3. C list: people you feel you "should" invite. Usually the first to cut.

Rules that make cuts easier

  • Plus-ones only for partners you've met.
  • Adults-only, or immediate family's children only.
  • Work colleagues: all of one team or none.
  • Each side gets the same number of A-list spots.

Questions

How do I decide who to cut from my wedding guest list?

Sort everyone into A (must have), B (would like) and C (feel obliged) lists, and cut from C first.

How much does each wedding guest cost?

Mostly catering and drinks. In our example about €121 per guest including invitations and favours.

Do I have to give every guest a plus-one?

No. A common rule is plus-ones only for married or long-term partners you have met.
