How to Make a Christmas Budget (With a Gift List Example)

Most Christmas overspending isn't one big purchase. It's forty small ones with no total behind them. A budget fixes the total first, then splits it.

Step 1: pick one total

Start from what you can actually pay: money already saved plus what you can put aside before December. Whatever is left after that goes on a card and follows you into January.

Step 2: split it into buckets

BucketExample

The small lines are the ones people forget: Secret Santa, teachers, wrapping, postage. Write them down now.

Step 4: track as you shop

Next to each line, add what you actually paid. If one gift goes over, take the difference from another line straight away. The total never moves.

Next year, without the stress: divide this year's total by 12 and save it monthly. €900 is €75 a month. Plan it with the free sinking funds calculator.

Questions

How much should I spend on Christmas?

Pick a total you can pay without a credit card balance in January. A common start is what you can save between now and December plus any money already set aside, not a percentage of income.

How do I make a Christmas gift list?

Write every person you buy for, give each a fixed amount, and add the real price next to it as you shop. When a gift costs more, take the difference from someone else's line, not from next month.

How do I save for next Christmas?

Divide this year's total by 12 and move that amount to a separate savings pot every month. €900 is €75 a month.
