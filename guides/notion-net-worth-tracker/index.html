Notion Net Worth Tracker: Build One in 10 Minutes (Formula Included)

Net worth is everything you own minus everything you owe. It is the one number that shows whether your money is moving forward, even in months when the budget felt messy. Here is how to track it in Notion with a single database and one formula.

Step 1: create the database

Add a table database called Net worth with these properties:

PropertyTypeWhat goes in
ItemTitleCurrent account, Savings, Car, Visa card, Car loan
TypeSelectAsset or Debt
ValueNumber (currency)Balance or resale value, always positive
UpdatedDateWhen you last checked the number
Counts asFormulaThe signed value (step 2)

Step 2: the formula

if(prop("Type") == "Debt", -prop("Value"), prop("Value"))
Debts become negative, assets stay positive. Set the column footer of Counts as to Sum and that sum is your net worth.

Keeping Value positive and flipping the sign in a formula means you can type balances exactly as your bank shows them.

What to include

  • Assets: cash and current accounts, savings, investments and pension (at today's value), the resale value of a car, home value if you own one.
  • Debts: credit cards, overdraft, car and personal loans, student loan, mortgage balance.
  • Skip things you would never sell (furniture, clothes). They make the number look better without changing anything.

Step 3: keep a monthly history

The table above always shows today. To see progress, add a second database called Snapshots with Month (title) and Net worth (number). On the first of each month, update the values, then copy the footer sum into a new Snapshots row. Twelve rows later you have a year of progress you can sort or chart.

How often to update

Once a month is enough. Daily changes are noise; the trend over 3 to 6 months is what matters. Put it on the same day as paying your bills so it becomes a habit.

Questions

How do I calculate net worth in Notion?

Make a database with Type (Asset or Debt) and Value, add the formula if(prop("Type") == "Debt", -prop("Value"), prop("Value")), and set the column footer to Sum.

Should I include my house in net worth?

Include its realistic market value as an asset and the mortgage balance as a debt. The difference is your home equity.

How often should I track net worth?

Monthly is enough. Look at the trend over several months rather than single changes.

Is negative net worth normal?

Yes, especially with student loans or a new car loan. What matters is that the number rises over time.
