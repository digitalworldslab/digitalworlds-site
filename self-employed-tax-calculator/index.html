FAQ

How much should I set aside for taxes when self-employed?

Many freelancers set aside 25–30% of profit. Self-employment tax alone is about 14.1% of profit (15.3% on 92.35%), plus federal and state income tax.

What is self-employment tax?

Social Security (12.4%) and Medicare (2.9%) that an employer would normally split with you. When you are self-employed you pay both halves, on 92.35% of net profit.

Do I need to pay quarterly estimated taxes?

Usually yes, if you expect to owe $1,000 or more for the year. Payments are due around mid-April, mid-June, mid-September and mid-January.

Is this tax advice?

No. It is a planning estimate to help you save enough. Your actual tax depends on deductions, credits and your state.
