FAQ

How much of my take-home pay should go on housing?

A common comfortable target is about 25% of take-home pay for the mortgage payment plus property tax and insurance. Up to about a third is a stretch that leaves less room for savings.

Why use take-home pay instead of gross?

Banks often lend on gross income, which can approve a payment that feels tight. Take-home pay is what you actually live on.

What else should I budget for?

Maintenance (often 1% of the price per year), utilities, moving costs and an emergency fund. Don't empty your savings for the deposit.
