FAQ

What is the unrecoverable cost of owning?

Money you pay as an owner and never get back: mortgage interest, property tax, insurance, maintenance and the return your deposit could have earned elsewhere. Mortgage principal is not counted, because it builds equity.

Is there a quick rule of thumb?

Add up those costs as a percentage of the home price, often around 4 to 6% a year. Multiply the price by that percentage and divide by 12: if your rent is lower, renting is usually cheaper. This calculator uses your own numbers instead of a fixed percentage.

What does this leave out?

Price growth, buying and selling fees, tax rules and how long you will stay. Buying usually makes more sense the longer you stay put.
