FAQ

How should I split my wedding budget?

A common starting point is 40–50% for venue, food and drinks, 10–12% for photo and video, around 8–10% each for attire and flowers, and about 10% kept back for surprises. Adjust to what matters most to you.

How do I lower the cost per guest?

Guest count drives most costs because food, drinks, chairs and invitations scale with it. Cutting 10 guests often saves more than any single vendor discount.

Should I keep a buffer?

Yes. Most couples go over budget somewhere. Keeping 5–10% aside avoids stress near the date.
