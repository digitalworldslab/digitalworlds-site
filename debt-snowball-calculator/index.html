FAQ

What is the debt snowball method?

Pay minimums on every debt and put every extra dollar on the smallest balance. When it is paid off, roll its payment into the next smallest.

What is the debt avalanche method?

Same idea, but the extra goes to the debt with the highest interest rate first. It usually costs less interest.

Which is better, snowball or avalanche?

Avalanche is cheaper on paper. Snowball gives faster wins, which helps many people stick with the plan. The calculator shows the difference for your debts.

Does the calculator keep my data?

No. Everything runs in your browser and nothing is sent or saved.
