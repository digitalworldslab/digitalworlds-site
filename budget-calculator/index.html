FAQ

What is the 50/30/20 rule?

Split your after-tax income: 50% for needs (housing, utilities, groceries, minimum debt payments), 30% for wants and 20% for savings and extra debt payoff.

Should I use gross or take-home pay?

Use take-home pay, the amount that reaches your bank account after tax and deductions.

What if my needs are more than 50%?

That is common with high rent. Cut wants first, then shrink the savings share for a while rather than skipping it.

How do I convert biweekly pay to monthly?

Multiply by 26 and divide by 12, about 2.17 times one paycheck. The calculator does this for you.
