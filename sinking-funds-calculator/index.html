FAQ

What is a sinking fund?

Money you set aside a little at a time for a known future expense, such as Christmas, car repairs or an annual insurance bill, so it never hits your budget all at once.

How do I calculate a sinking fund?

Take the amount you still need, divide it by the number of months until you need it. That is your monthly contribution.

What sinking funds should I have?

Common ones: Christmas and gifts, car maintenance, car insurance, medical, home repairs, vacation, back to school, pet care and annual subscriptions.

Sinking fund vs emergency fund?

An emergency fund is for surprises. A sinking fund is for expenses you know are coming.
